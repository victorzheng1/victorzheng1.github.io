Here is a display of my work from school or fun!

Child detector made in design class using FSR, temperature, and PIR sensors, and Arduino UNO to detect temperature and the variables that corresponded to a presence of a child (weight and movement). This was programmed with Arduino C and assembled over a course of a few months. It was fully functional in the end;it was able to sense the conditions and deliver the info to server as well as triggering the buzzer upon dangerous conditions.

Bio

Raised in the vibrant and diverse immigrant community in the neighbourhood of Flemingdon Park in Toronto. I was immersed in a rich multicultural settings with people of all backgrounds. From an immigrant background of lower socioeconomic status, I have a great appreciation for industrious work ethic and know firsthand how impactful and important social ventures and a benevolent government are. I learned how  important it is to give back to your community especially the marginalized minorities who struggle even more. I learned how important EDII principles are and how adhering can positively uplift those around you.

I am also a firm advocate of animal welfare! I am the occasional foster parent of canine and feline friends! And a proud father of one red eared slider!

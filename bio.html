Bio

I come from an impoverished family from one of China's poorest provinces in community centered around a tobacco factory. My family came to Canada with my grandparents' savings and was raised up in Flemingdon Park, one of Toronto's most vibrant and diverse immigrant neighbourhoods. From this upbringing, I was immersed in different cultures around me, from Afghan, Somali, and Moldovan.  The community and its challenges created the person who I am today, and I am forever grateful; this is why I stay committed to giving back to my community and country.

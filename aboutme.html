About Me!

Being raised up in Toronto, I love almost any food! From the savoury samosa store across the street to the enchanting enchiladas down the road, I, like many other Torontoians, am proud to call my self a foodie! With my guilty pleasures, I like to offset my indulgence with my weekly fitness routine (yes I do rack my weights to where they belong). I am a huge football (you know the one that's actually played with your foot) fan, and an avid supporter of Tottenham Hotspur (levy out).
